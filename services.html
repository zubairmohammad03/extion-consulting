Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to know exactly what service I need?

No. Tell us about your business, where you are today, and what you're trying to achieve. We'll help you identify the right starting point.

Can you work with an idea that hasn't become a business yet?

Yes. Our Get Started services help you test your assumptions, understand your market, design the right business model, and create a practical path to market before you invest heavily.

Do you work with small businesses and MSMEs?

Yes. We work with entrepreneurs, startups, and MSMEs at every stage, from validating an idea to preparing to scale.

Do you only provide recommendations, or help with implementation too?

Both. Through our Build & Implement engagement we provide hands-on support translating strategy into processes, systems, and operational capabilities.

How do you determine what a business actually needs?

We start by understanding your business, context, customers, ambitions, and challenges, then diagnose the underlying problems and opportunities before recommending anything.

How long does an engagement typically take?

It depends on the engagement model. A focused intervention is shorter than a strategic engagement or ongoing advisory. We agree on a timeline with you once we understand what you need.

Can we continue working with you after a project is completed?

Yes. Our Ongoing Advisory engagement gives you continued access to strategic thinking, business expertise, and an external perspective as your business evolves.
